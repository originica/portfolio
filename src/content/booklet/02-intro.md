I'm Olivia, a full-stack engineer who believes good software makes life easier for the people who use it, and for the developers who build on it.

Since November 2023, I've worked across the full stack, on frontend, backend, and end-to-end projects alike. Some I built from the ground up; others meant stepping into existing codebases, from small applications to large-scale systems connected to many other services. My work often extends past the application itself, into deployment and automation.

The part of the work I care about most is quality: clear, maintainable code, thoughtful reviews, and testing that catches problems before users do.
